Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our AI tools and services

What is AI Power Tools?

AI Power Tools is a collection of AI-powered generators that help you create high-quality content quickly and easily. Our tools include tweet generators, LinkedIn bio creators, video script writers, and resume builders - all powered by artificial intelligence.

Are these tools really free to use?

Yes! All our AI tools are completely free to use with no hidden costs or subscriptions. We believe powerful AI tools should be accessible to everyone. In the future, we may offer premium features, but the core functionality will always remain free.

Do I need to create an account?

No account or registration is required. You can start using our tools immediately without providing any personal information. Everything works right in your browser.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser - we don't store your inputs or the generated outputs on our servers. Your content never leaves your device unless you choose to save or share it.

Can I use the generated content commercially?

Yes, all content generated by our tools is yours to use however you see fit, including for commercial purposes. We don't claim any ownership over the content you create with our tools.

How accurate are the AI-generated results?

Our AI models produce high-quality, human-like content. However, we always recommend reviewing and editing the output to ensure it meets your specific needs. The more detailed your input, the better the results will be.

Which browsers are supported?

Our tools work on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, we recommend using the latest version of your browser.

Do the tools work on mobile devices?

Yes! All our tools are fully responsive and work perfectly on smartphones and tablets. You can create content on the go from any device.

Can I request new features or tools?

We'd love to hear your suggestions! Please contact us with your ideas for new features or tools you'd like to see added to our platform.

What should I do if a tool isn't working?

First, try refreshing the page and ensuring you're using a supported browser. If the problem persists, please contact us with details about the issue you're experiencing, including any error messages you see.
